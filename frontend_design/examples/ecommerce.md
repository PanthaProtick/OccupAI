# E-commerce Design Example

## Goal

Help users discover, evaluate, and purchase products.

---

## Structure

```text
Header
Search
Categories
Hero / promotion
Filters
Product grid
Product detail
Cart
Checkout
```

---

## Product Card

Should communicate:

```text
Image
Product name
Price
Rating where relevant
Important badge
Quick action where useful
```

Do not overcrowd the card.

---

## Product Detail

Prioritize:

```text
Images
Product name
Price
Availability
Options
CTA
Description
Reviews
Related products
```

---

## Animation

Good:

```text
Image transitions
Gallery interaction
Add-to-cart feedback
Filter transitions
Wishlist feedback
```

Avoid slow transitions that interfere with shopping.

---

## Color

Product photography should remain dominant.

UI colors should support rather than compete with products.

---

## Mobile

Prioritize:

```text
Search
Product image
Price
CTA
```

Keep touch interactions easy.
