OccupAI Assistant: Supported Query Types

The assistant answers grounded campus-room questions using fresh, reliable
OccupAI occupancy data.

1. Available or free rooms
   - Which rooms are available right now?
   - Find free rooms.
   - Suggest me a room.
   - Recommend a quiet space.

2. Quietest or least-occupied rooms
   - What are the three quietest rooms?
   - Show the least occupied rooms.
   - Find rooms below 40% occupancy.

3. Rooms for a group
   - Which rooms can fit 20 people?
   - Find a room for 25 people.
   - Show rooms with at least 30 available seats.

4. Rooms on a specific floor
   - Show rooms on the Ground Floor.
   - Find rooms on Floor 2.
   - Which rooms are on the 5th floor?

5. Rooms in a specific block
   - Show rooms in A Block.
   - Find available rooms in C Block.

6. Rooms in a specific building
   - Find rooms in the University Building.

7. Nearby alternatives
   - Suggest an alternative to room 2A03.
   - Find a nearby quiet room instead of 2A03.
   - Avoid crowded rooms near 2A03.

8. Crowded or busiest rooms
   - Which rooms are crowded?
   - What are the busiest rooms?
   - Which rooms are above 80% occupied?

9. Occupancy thresholds
   - Show rooms below 40% occupancy.
   - Which rooms are over 70% occupied?
   - Find rooms with at most 30% occupancy.

10. Room status and availability
    - Is room 2A03 available?
    - What is the occupancy of room 2A03?
    - How full is room 2A03?

11. Room comparisons
    - Compare 2A03 and 2B04.
    - What is the difference between room 2A03 and room 2B04?

12. Ranking and result limits
    - Show the top 3 quiet rooms.
    - Find the best 5 available rooms.
    - Which room has the most free capacity?

Queries can combine filters such as floor, block, building, capacity,
occupancy percentage, and result count.

Scope limitations

- Results use only fresh, reliable, online occupancy readings.
- Offline, stale, disabled, missing, or inconsistent readings are excluded.
- The assistant does not answer unrelated requests or invent room information.
- The optional Gemini provider only improves wording; it cannot select rooms
  or override the verified results.
